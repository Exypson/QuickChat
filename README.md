# QuickChat

**QuickChat** is a sleek, intuitive radial chat mod for *R.E.P.O.* designed for quick tactical callouts and seamless communication. 

A lot of us have that one friend who can't use a mic at times, or sometimes *we* are the mute friend. When the Headman is gliding down the hallway towards you with murderous intent, standing still to open the chat box and furiously breaking it down on your keyboard to warn your teammates—or beg the guy carrying the shotgun for help—is almost never a good survival strategy. You'd be dead by the time you finished typing. 

And thus, I bring you guys: **QuickChat**. Instead of slowly typing your life away, you can simply hold a hotkey (`V` by default) to bring up a quick chat wheel. Flick your mouse towards an option and release the key, and your character will instantly speak out loud whatever is assigned in the menu. 

## Features:
* **Fluid Radial Menu:** Simply hold `V`, aim your mouse at an option, and release the hotkey to send the message. It's incredibly fast and won't disrupt your gameplay.
* **Eight Essential Comms:** The default wheel comes equipped with the essentials: *Yes, No, Help, Hide, Follow me, Thanks, Run,* and *Stop*.
* **Full Customizability:** Don't like the defaults? You can easily create your own custom chat options in the wheel. Full in-game customization available.

## Installation:
1. Ensure you have all the required dependencies installed.
2. Download the latest `QuickChat.zip` and extract the .dll file in your `BepInEx\plugins` folder.
3. Launch the game!
